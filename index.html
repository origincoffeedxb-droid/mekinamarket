    Questions buyers ask

    Does it cost anything to request a part?

    No. Posting a request is free for buyers.

    What happens after I send a request?

    Suppliers who stock the part contact you on the number you gave. Compare their offers before you decide.

    Does Mekina Market sell the parts?

    No. We connect buyers with suppliers. You buy from the supplier you choose.

    I only have a photo or a part number. Can I still ask?

    Yes. Type the part number in the details box. When a supplier contacts you, send the photo in your chat.

    How do I stay safe when paying?

    Ask for photos or a video of the exact part, check it in person when you can, and pay only when you are satisfied.
